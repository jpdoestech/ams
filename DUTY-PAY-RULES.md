## 2. How it works
- **Classification** (`dayCode`): regular holidays ⇒ `RH` (`DRH` if two *different* regular holidays coincide); special non-working ⇒ `SNWD`/`DSNWD`; special working ⇒ `SWD`; the employee's assigned rest day adds `-RD` (or `RD` on an otherwise ordinary day). Regular beats special if they coincide (assumption).
- **Engine** (`calcDuty`, pure function): every worked minute (breaks removed) is allocated to its calendar date, so a shift crossing midnight into a holiday splits into two segments. The first *normal hours* (default 8, per employee) are regular, later minutes are OT; minutes between 22:00 and 06:00 are night hours.
- **Amounts** (per calendar-day segment, each component rounded to centavos): `hourly = daily rate ÷ normal hours`; regular = hourly × regular hrs × regular multiplier (shown as base ×1.00 plus premium ×(m−1)); OT = hourly × OT hrs × OT multiplier; **NSD = hourly × night hrs × applicable (regular or OT) multiplier × NSD %**.
- **Rule version**: `ruleFor(code, workDate)` picks the latest active version effective on the work date; new versions never alter old ones; values below the DOLE minimum or NSD < 10% are rejected.
- **Regular holiday not worked**: paid `unworked_pct` of the daily rate only if the employee is covered, and not absent/AWOL/unpaid-leave on the previous working day (rest days and non-working holidays are skipped backwards). Special non-working not worked: no pay (0%) unless you raise it in a rule version.
- **Rest day not worked / empty rest-day cell**: day-off, not paid.
- **N/A**: a day before `date_hired` or after `date_end` is virtual N/A (not stored, cannot be edited); `N/A` can also be set manually. Applicable days = calendar days − N/A. Example: hired Oct 10, cut-off Oct 1–15 ⇒ 9 N/A days, 6 applicable, absences analysed on those 6 only.

## 5. Assumptions and open items (decide before payroll use)
1. **Effective-dated wages** are not modelled: one current daily rate per employee; each saved day keeps a wage snapshot in `attendance_pay.comps`. A `wage_history` table is the next step.
2. Rules are company-wide (`scope='company'`); branch/client scoping and CBA overrides beyond new rule versions are not built.
3. Break position: schedule break blocks are used when the break equals the schedule; otherwise a centred break is assumed. It matters only for night-hour allocation.
4. Regular + special coincidence uses the regular holiday. `RH-RD` unworked pay defaults to 100%: confirm with DOLE guidance/CBA.
5. Monthly-paid vs daily-paid divisors, 13th-month, leave pay and deductions are out of scope; LEAVE = paid, LEAVE-U = unpaid.
6. No half-day or multi-shift-per-day or concurrent-edit conflict detection (last write wins, audited).
7. Verify against: DOLE/NWPC *Handbook on Workers' Statutory Monetary Benefits* (2024, computations appendix), Labor Code Book III, current DOLE labor advisories and official holiday proclamations.
